Methodological Guidelines for Fair Empirical Evaluation

When evaluating model performance in external examinations:
• Regression Models (EXP 02, 03, 13): Must be evaluated using $R^2$ Score (Coefficient of Determination), Mean Squared Error (MSE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and Mean Absolute Error (MAE).
• Classification Models (EXP 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 12): Must be evaluated using Confusion Matrix, Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC.
• Split Integrity: Models can only be compared fairly when evaluated on an identical, uncorrupted train-test split (e.g. random_state=42 with 80% train / 20% test).
